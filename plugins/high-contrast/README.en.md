# High contrast

A high-contrast theme for Escouade, dark and light: pure text, sharp borders, plain status colors, and six project colors that stand apart.

**Who it is for**: if Escouade’s grey text or thin borders tire your eyes, on a bright screen, in daylight, or if you need more contrast to read.

**What it needs**: nothing. No account, no secret, no network, no program.

**What it can do**: give colors to Escouade’s interface. It is declarative: nothing of it runs, Escouade reads its colors and filters them before setting them.

Its ratios, checked by its tests on the background, the raised surfaces and the terminals’ background (`--frame`, `--raise`, `--raise-2`, `--well`), dark and light:

- text at 7:1 at least (WCAG AAA);
- the accent and the status colors at 4.5:1 at least;
- borders at 3:1 at least.
