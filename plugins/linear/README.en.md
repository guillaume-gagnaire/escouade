# Linear

The issues of a Linear team in Escouade's Kanban, its workflow states as columns. You link a team to a project, import its ongoing issues as tickets, and each issue follows its ticket: when the ticket changes column, the issue moves to the chosen state, and Escouade can leave a comment on it.

## Who it is for

For you if your team tracks its work in Linear and you want to hand those issues to agents without copying them.

## What it needs

- A Linear account and a personal API key, made in the security settings of your Linear account.
- Nothing to install on your machine.

## What it can do

- Reach `api.linear.app`, and no other host.
- Receive from you your account's API key, which Escouade keeps in the system keychain.
- Read your teams, their workflow states and their issues that are neither completed nor canceled; change an issue's state; comment on an issue.
- Nothing else: it runs no program, reads no file and receives nothing of what your agents do, apart from the text of the comments Escouade asks it to post.

## Learn more

Its documentation has five tabs: **Install** (connect it step by step), **Use** (what it does, screen by screen), **Permissions** (what it reads, writes and sends), **Troubleshooting** (its messages) and **Develop** (for whoever wants to read or change its code).
